How the cost of owning is built. It adds interest paid, property tax, insurance, HOA or condo fees, closing costs, the special assessment, and repairs over the comparison period. It leaves out principal paydown and appreciation. Principal builds equity rather than costing you money, and appreciation depends on the market. Use the total to compare homes side by side, then weigh location and appreciation yourself.

Property tax is a percentage of price per year, so divide your annual tax bill by the price. Check whether a homestead exemption applies if you will live in the home. Florida's homestead exemption requires owning and living in the home on January 1, and applications are due by March 1. This tool produces estimates for comparison. It is not a quote, and it is not legal, tax, or insurance advice.
